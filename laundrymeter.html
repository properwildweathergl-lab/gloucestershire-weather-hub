🌬️ Pro Drying & Pegging Tips

  • Spacing is Key: Leave at least one hand's width between garments to let the breeze circulate freely.
  • Heavy Items Low: Hang jeans and towels on the outer lines where weight is supported best, keeping shirts and lighter fabrics closer to the center.
  • Turn Inside Out: Protect dark clothes and bright colors from UV fading while sun-drying.

🧷 The Best Pegs for Gloucestershire Breezes

  • Soft-Grip Plastic Pegs: Gentle on fine cottons and synthetics without leaving pinch marks.
  • Marine-Grade Stainless Steel Pegs: Totally rust-proof, unbreakable, and won't snap under heavy wind gusts across the open fields.
