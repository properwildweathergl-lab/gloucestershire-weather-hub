🛡️ Severe Weather Safety Protocols

  • High Winds: Secure loose garden items, trampolines, and bins. Avoid parking vehicles under mature trees.
  • Heavy Rain & Flooding: Never drive or walk through moving floodwater. Keep local floodline numbers handy.
  • Thunderstorms: Unplug sensitive electronics and stay away from open windows during close electrical activity.
